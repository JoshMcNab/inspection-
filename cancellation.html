Cancellation Information

Effective 10 September 2026 · Version UAW-CANCEL-2026-09-10-1

Quote requests are not work authorisation

Submitting a quote request does not authorise repairs and does not by itself create a workshop service contract. A contract normally arises when you accept a quote or otherwise instruct us to carry out work.

Your cancellation rights

If a consumer contract is made entirely at a distance or away from the workshop and the statutory cancellation rules apply, you will normally have a 14-day cancellation period for the service contract. The exact position can depend on how and where the contract was made and on the type of work.

This information does not reduce any cancellation or refund rights you have under law.

If you want work to start within the cancellation period

When approving a quote online you can expressly request that we are allowed to start the service before any applicable 14-day cancellation period has ended.

If you make that request and then cancel after work has started but before the service is fully completed, you may have to pay a reasonable amount proportionate to the service already supplied.

If the service is fully completed during the cancellation period after your express request to start early, and after you acknowledge that full performance can end the cancellation right, you may lose the statutory right to cancel that completed service.

If you do not request an early start

Where the statutory cancellation rules apply, we should not rely on an online approval alone as a request to begin the service during the cancellation period. If you want work to begin sooner, use the separate early-start option shown on the approval page or give the workshop a clear written request.

How to cancel

Tell Ultimate Automotive Works clearly that you want to cancel. Contact the workshop using the contact details on your quote, invoice, booking confirmation or other workshop communication. Keep a copy of your message and include your name, vehicle registration and quote/job reference where available.

You can use the wording below, but you do not have to use this exact form:

To: Ultimate Automotive Works I give notice that I wish to cancel my contract for the following vehicle service/work: Vehicle registration: Quote or job reference: Customer name: Customer address: Date contract/quote was accepted: Date of cancellation: Signed (only if sent on paper):

Work, parts or costs already committed

Where cancellation occurs after authorised work has started, or after specifically authorised non-returnable/special-order items have been committed, what is payable will depend on the circumstances and the law. We will not use this policy to impose an unfair penalty or remove statutory rights.

Need help?

If you are unsure whether a cancellation right applies, contact us before work starts. You can also obtain independent consumer advice from Citizens Advice.
