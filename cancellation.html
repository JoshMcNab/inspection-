Quote requests are not work authorisation

Submitting a quote request does not authorise repairs and does not by itself create a workshop service contract. A contract normally arises when you accept a quote or otherwise instruct us to carry out work.

Your cancellation rights

If a consumer service contract is made entirely at a distance or away from the workshop and the statutory cancellation rules apply, you will normally have a 14-day cancellation period starting when the service contract is made. The exact position can depend on how and where the contract was made and on the type of work or circumstances.

This information does not reduce any cancellation, refund or other consumer rights you have under law.

If you want work to start within the cancellation period

When approving a quote online you can expressly request that we may start the service before any applicable 14-day cancellation period has ended.

If you make that request and then validly cancel after work has started but before the service is fully completed, you may have to pay a reasonable amount proportionate to the service already supplied.

If the service is fully completed during the cancellation period after your express request to start early, and after the acknowledgement required by law, the statutory right to cancel that completed service may end where the law permits.

If you do not request an early start

Where the statutory cancellation rules apply, we will not treat online quote approval by itself as an express request to begin the service during the cancellation period. If you want work to begin sooner, select the separate early-start option on the approval page or give the workshop a clear written request.

How to cancel

Tell Ultimate Automotive Works LTD clearly that you want to cancel. You can use the workshop contact details shown on your quote, invoice, booking confirmation or other workshop communication, or write to our registered office at 9 Water Fir Drive, Harworth, Doncaster, England, DN11 8ND. Keep a copy of your message and include your name, vehicle registration and quote/job reference where available.

You can use the wording below, but you do not have to use this exact form:

To: Ultimate Automotive Works LTD 9 Water Fir Drive Harworth Doncaster England DN11 8ND I give notice that I wish to cancel my contract for the following vehicle service/work: Vehicle registration: Quote or job reference: Customer name: Customer address: Date contract/quote was accepted: Date of cancellation: Signed (only if sent on paper):

Work, parts or costs already committed

Where cancellation occurs after authorised work has started, or after specifically authorised non-returnable or special-order items have been committed, what is payable will depend on the circumstances and the law. We will not use this policy to impose an unfair penalty or remove statutory rights.

Need help?

If you are unsure whether a cancellation right applies, contact us before work starts. You can also obtain independent consumer advice from Citizens Advice.
