Complaints Procedure

Effective 10 September 2026 · Version UAW-COMPLAINTS-2026-09-10-1

We want the opportunity to put things right

If you are unhappy with a quote, repair, inspection, communication, invoice or other part of our service, please contact Ultimate Automotive Works as soon as reasonably possible so the issue can be investigated.

How to make a complaint

Use the workshop contact details shown on your quote, invoice, booking confirmation or other workshop communication. To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your name and preferred contact details.
  • Vehicle registration and, if known, the job or quote reference.
  • A clear description of what has happened and when.
  • What outcome you are asking us to consider.
  • Any relevant photographs, documents or messages.

What happens next

We will review the available job records, approvals, inspection information and communications, ask for any further information reasonably needed, and respond as soon as reasonably possible. Where appropriate, we may ask to inspect the vehicle before deciding what remedy is suitable.

Please avoid authorising another repairer to alter the disputed work before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless that is necessary for safety or to prevent further loss.

If the complaint is not resolved

You may seek independent consumer advice from Citizens Advice. Where an Alternative Dispute Resolution scheme is legally required or one is agreed to be used, we will provide the relevant details.

Using this complaints procedure does not limit your statutory rights or your right to seek independent legal advice.

Data used during a complaint

Complaint records are handled in accordance with our Privacy Notice and may be retained where reasonably necessary for customer service, warranty, insurance, legal or regulatory purposes.
