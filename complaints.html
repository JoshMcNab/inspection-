Complaints Procedure

Effective 10 September 2026 · Version UAW-COMPLAINTS-2026-09-10-3

Who to complain to

Ultimate Automotive Works LTD, company number 14995808, registered in England and Wales. Registered office: 9 Water Fir Drive, Harworth, Doncaster, England, DN11 8ND.
Email: ultimateautomotiveworksinquiry@gmail.com

If you are unhappy with a quote, repair, inspection, communication, invoice or other part of our service, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible so we have an opportunity to investigate and put matters right where appropriate.

How to make a complaint

Email ultimateautomotiveworksinquiry@gmail.com or write to the registered office above. To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your name and preferred contact details.
  • Vehicle registration and, if known, the job or quote reference.
  • A clear description of what happened and when.
  • What outcome you are asking us to consider.
  • Any relevant photographs, documents or messages.

What happens next

We will acknowledge and investigate the complaint as soon as reasonably possible. We may review job records, approvals, inspection information and communications and ask for further information reasonably needed to assess the issue.

Where appropriate, we may ask to inspect the vehicle before deciding what remedy is suitable. Please avoid authorising another repairer to alter disputed work before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless that is necessary for safety or to prevent further loss.

If the complaint is not resolved

You may seek independent consumer advice from Citizens Advice. If an Alternative Dispute Resolution scheme becomes legally required for the dispute, or we agree to use one, we will provide the relevant details.

Using this complaints procedure does not limit your statutory rights or your right to seek independent legal advice.

Data-protection complaints

If your complaint concerns how we use personal information, please identify it as a data-protection complaint. We will handle it under our Privacy Notice. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
