1. When these terms apply

These terms apply when a customer instructs Ultimate Automotive Works LTD to inspect, diagnose, maintain or repair a vehicle, including when a quote is approved online. Sending a quote request by itself is only a request for an estimate and does not authorise work.

Nothing in these terms removes or reduces rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under UK consumer law.

2. Quotes, estimates and authorisation

A fixed quote is the agreed price for the work specifically described, subject to any clearly stated assumptions. An estimate or price range is our reasonable indication of likely cost and may change if the vehicle condition or required work differs from what could reasonably be identified beforehand.

Online approval authorises the work shown in that quote up to the displayed authorised amount. We will seek further approval before carrying out additional chargeable work outside that scope or authorised limit, unless the customer separately instructs us.

Where VAT or another tax is shown on a quote or invoice, it forms part of the displayed total.

3. Diagnostics and hidden faults

Vehicle faults can have more than one cause and some defects only become apparent after testing, dismantling or completing earlier work. A diagnostic charge may still be payable where it was agreed even if further repair is recommended. If additional work is needed, we will normally provide a revised quote or obtain separate authority before proceeding.

4. Parts and materials

Parts supplied by us will be suitable for the agreed repair and may be genuine, OEM-equivalent, reconditioned or other quality parts where this has been agreed or clearly described. Availability can affect timing.

If a customer asks us to fit customer-supplied parts, we may refuse parts that appear unsafe, unsuitable or incompatible. We are not responsible for a defect inherent in a customer-supplied part where that defect was not caused by us, but this does not remove responsibility for our own workmanship or any statutory rights.

5. Vehicle testing and handling

Where reasonably necessary to diagnose a fault, confirm a repair, complete an inspection or verify safety, the customer authorises reasonable workshop movement and road testing of the vehicle by an appropriately authorised person.

Please remove valuables and personal items where possible. We will take reasonable care of the vehicle and property left with it.

6. Completion times

Any completion date or time is an estimate unless we expressly agree that it is fixed. Delays can arise from parts supply, further faults, specialist work or circumstances outside reasonable control. We will provide updates where a material delay becomes known.

7. Payment, collection and storage

Payment is due as agreed on the quote, invoice or other workshop communication. Unless another arrangement has been agreed, the vehicle should be collected promptly after the customer is told it is ready.

Any storage charge will only be applied where the basis, amount and point from which it may arise have been clearly notified in advance and the charge is fair and reasonable in the circumstances.

8. Replaced parts

If you want replaced parts returned, tell us before work starts. Some parts cannot be returned because of safety, environmental, warranty, exchange or supplier-return requirements. Where parts are not returned to the customer, they may be retained, recycled or disposed of responsibly.

9. Cancellations

If the contract is made remotely or away from the workshop, statutory cancellation rights may apply. Please read our Cancellation Information before approving a quote online.

If work has started at your express request during an applicable cancellation period and you then cancel before completion, you may have to pay a reasonable amount for the service already supplied. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill. Parts and any specific warranty are also subject to the rights that apply by law. If you believe there is a problem with our work, contact us promptly so we can investigate and, where appropriate, put matters right.
