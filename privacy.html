Privacy Notice

Effective 10 September 2026 · Version UAW-PRIVACY-2026-09-10-1

Who is responsible for your information

Ultimate Automotive Works, or the legal business entity identified on your quote, invoice or booking confirmation, is responsible for customer personal information handled through this website and workshop system.

For privacy requests, contact the workshop using the contact details shown on your quote, invoice, booking confirmation or other workshop communication.

Information we may collect

  • Your name, address, phone number, email address and preferred contact method.
  • Vehicle registration, make/model, mileage and other vehicle or job information.
  • Quote requests, bookings, inspection results, photographs, notes, invoices, payment records and warranty information.
  • Electronic quote approvals, signatures, authorised amounts, dates and times.
  • Messages and records of communications with the workshop.
  • Limited technical and security information needed to operate and protect the customer portal and website.

Who we may share information with

We may use trusted service providers that host or support the workshop system, data storage, communications and payments. Information may also be shared with professional advisers, insurers, suppliers or authorities where reasonably necessary, required by law, or needed to deal with a claim or customer request.

We do not sell customer personal information.

Where information is processed

Our service providers may process information in the UK, the EEA and, depending on the provider, other countries. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we expect the relevant lawful transfer mechanism to be used.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and vehicle information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including servicing history, warranties, accounting, tax, legal claims, safety and dispute handling. Financial or transactional records may be kept for periods required by law. Other information is reviewed and should be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain uses. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Visit ico.org.uk for current complaint information.

Cookies and browser storage

The customer-facing pages are designed without advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Essential browser storage, secure tokens or similar technologies may be used where needed to provide requested features, security, reliability or offline behaviour. If non-essential analytics or marketing technologies are introduced later, this notice and any consent controls should be updated first.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date and version at the top identify the notice in force.
