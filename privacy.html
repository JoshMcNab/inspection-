Who we may share information with

We may use trusted service providers that host or support our website, workshop database, file storage, communications, backups and payments. We may also share relevant information with suppliers, subcontractors, professional advisers, insurers, payment providers, fraud-prevention services or public authorities where reasonably necessary, requested by you, required by law, or needed to deal with a claim.

We do not sell customer personal information.

International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we use or expect our providers to use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism.

How long we keep information

Retention depends on why the information is needed. Our normal approach is:

  • Quote requests that do not proceed: normally up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact, unless a longer period is needed for a complaint, claim, security issue or legal requirement.
  • Completed jobs, quotes, approvals, inspections, service history and related customer records: normally up to 6 years after the relevant job or customer relationship ends, with longer retention only where reasonably required for a warranty, legal obligation or claim.
  • Accounting and tax records: for the period required by applicable law.
  • Technical and security records: normally up to 12 months unless needed for an active security investigation.
  • Marketing preferences: until you withdraw them or they are no longer reasonably current.

Photographs and signatures form part of the relevant quote, job or approval record and follow the retention period for that record. Information should be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer reasonably needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain uses. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Visit ico.org.uk for current complaint information.

Automated decisions

We do not use this workshop system to make solely automated decisions about customers that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies and browser storage

The customer-facing pages are designed without advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Essential browser storage, secure tokens or similar technologies may be used where needed to provide requested features, security, reliability or offline behaviour. If non-essential analytics or marketing technologies are introduced later, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated first.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or legal requirements change. The effective date and version at the top identify the notice in force. Where a material change affects how existing customer information is used, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to customers' attention where required.
